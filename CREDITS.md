The raised-fist glyph used by the favourite button (`src/components/FistButton.tsx`)
is the `hand-fist` icon (formerly `fist-raised`) from
[Font Awesome Free 6.7.2](https://fontawesome.com/icons/hand-fist).

dependency, so the PWA stays fully offline and light. The path itself is
unmodified.
